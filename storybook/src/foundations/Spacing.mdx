# Spacing
Figma page `➜ Corner Radius & Spacing`. `space/{n}` = n × 4px on Desktop. `space/0` to `space/4` never change; `space/5` and up compress on iPad and Mobile (switch **Mode** in the toolbar). Tailwind: `p-{n}`, `gap-{n}`.
